Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy describes how the 무명야담 Uploader (“the App”), an internal tool of the 무명야담 channel (YouTube @nonamestory-kr), handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App is used only by the channel operator with the operator's own TikTok account. When the operator authorizes the App, TikTok issues an access token and basic account information (such as the account's open ID and display name). The App collects nothing else and does not collect information about any other TikTok user or viewer.

2. How we use it

The access token is used only to upload the channel's own videos and captions to the operator's own TikTok account and to check the status of those uploads.

3. Storage

The token is stored only on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to any third party other than TikTok's API.

4. Deletion

The operator can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings; the locally stored token is then deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the channel operator.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
